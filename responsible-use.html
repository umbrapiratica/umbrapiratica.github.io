Responsible Use

Regent Innovations develops cybersecurity research and software for defensive, educational, and explicitly authorized security-assessment purposes.

Authorization first

Security testing, validation, and research must be limited to systems that are owned by the tester, purpose-built lab environments, or systems for which the tester has clear authorization from the responsible organization.

Risk controls

Our design direction favors passive and non-destructive methods when they can answer the relevant security question. Higher-impact validation should be constrained by target scope, action policy, human approval, monitoring, stop conditions, and cleanup requirements.

Evidence and accountability

Security conclusions should be supported by observable evidence and reproducible verification. We aim to preserve enough context to explain what was observed, why a decision was made, what changed, and whether remediation actually closed the exposure.

Prohibited intent

Regent Innovations does not support unauthorized access, destructive activity, indiscriminate exploitation, persistence on third-party systems, or activity outside an approved rules-of-engagement boundary.

Security contact

For security-related questions, research collaboration, or responsible disclosure concerning Regent Innovations, contact security@regentinnovations.com.
